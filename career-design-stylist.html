Floral & Design Stylist

You will shape how a room feels — florals, tabletop, lighting cues and the small gestures that make a celebration look as considered as it is planned.

The role

Design at Pearls360 is never a template. This role works beside the Head Design Architect to build concepts, source materials and install environments that photograph beautifully and feel personal in person.

What you will do

  • Develop mood, palette and floral language for weddings, galas and private dinners.
  • Style tables, ceremony structures, lounges and welcome moments.
  • Source vessels, linens, candles and specialty pieces with our vendor partners.
  • Lead install days with a small crew, then strike with the same care.
  • Support editorial and proposal moments when the studio needs a quieter set.

What you bring

  • A strong floral or event-styling portfolio.
  • Experience installing on site, including long days and weekend work.
  • An eye for proportion, color and how guests move through a space.
  • A collaborative temperament — design here is a conversation, not a solo act.
