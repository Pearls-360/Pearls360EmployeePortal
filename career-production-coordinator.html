Production Coordinator

You will keep every moving part in time — load-in, vendors, rehearsals and guest flow — so the celebration can feel unhurried even when the production is complex.

The role

Extraordinary rooms only work when logistics are invisible. This role supports planners and designers by holding the production plan: schedules, site constraints, rentals, and the sequence of the day.

What you will do

  • Build production schedules, load-in plans and vendor call sheets.
  • Walk venues with the planning team and flag what will affect design or guest flow.
  • Manage rental inventories, deliveries and on-site crew.
  • Support destination celebrations with travel, lodging and local vendor coordination.
  • Remain the steady point of contact on event day for vendors and the studio.

What you bring

  • Three or more years in event production, hospitality operations or luxury planning support.
  • Comfort with spreadsheets, floor plans and last-minute changes.
  • A calm presence under pressure and a respect for client privacy.
  • A valid driver’s license and willingness to work evenings and weekends.
