Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2025

Pearls360 Design & Planning (“Pearls360”, “we”, “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit our website or contact us, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

  • Information you provide — your name, email address, phone number, event date, location, guest count and any details you share through our inquiry form, by email or by phone.
  • Usage information — standard technical data such as browser type, device, pages visited and referring URLs, collected through server logs and embedded third-party media players.

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your inquiry and discuss your celebration.
  • To prepare proposals, contracts and planning documents.
  • To improve our website and services.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with trusted vendors and partners who help us deliver your event, and only as needed to do so.

Third-Party Content

Our website embeds media from services such as Google Fonts, YouTube and Vimeo. These providers may collect information in accordance with their own privacy policies when the embedded content loads.

Photography

Images and films of past events are shared with the permission of our clients. If you appear in any content on this website and would like it removed, please contact us and we will do so promptly.

Your Choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information we hold about you at any time by emailing Info@pearls360design.com.
