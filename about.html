The Experience Architect

Born from Love

Nifé was not the child sketching tablescapes, planning imaginary weddings, or decorating every room in sight. Pearls360 was born from love.

In her early twenties, Nifé found herself wanting to celebrate someone she deeply cared about in a way that felt different from the ordinary. Dinner had been done. Movies had been done. The thoughtful dates, sweet gestures, and little adventures that come with loving someone had all been experienced. She wanted something more. Something meaningful. Something intentional. Something unforgettable.

So she began researching. The food. The ambiance. The experience. The surprises. The details that make someone feel seen, celebrated, honored, and loved. She designed the entire evening from start to finish. She created a four-course meal, leaned on the wisdom of her aunts to learn recipes she had never prepared before, carefully selected meaningful gifts, and transformed an ordinary space into an unforgettable experience. Every detail had purpose. Every decision was intentional. When he walked into the room, he was completely surprised.

What she created became more than a celebration. It became a memory. A feeling. A story that would live long beyond the evening itself. After sharing photos of the experience with friends and family, they saw something she had not yet recognized in herself. They saw an ability to create moments that moved people. Soon, friends began asking for help with birthdays, celebrations, and special occasions. Then family members followed. Then referrals followed after that. What began as acts of love for the people around her slowly evolved into something much bigger than she ever imagined. Eventually, Pearls360 was born.

As the company grew, so did the philosophy behind it. Nifé’s approach to event architecture was shaped by an uncommon combination of experiences across luxury service, accounting, banking, technology, hospitality, and business operations. Through luxury environments, she developed a deep understanding of elevated client experiences and world-class service. Through her academic foundation in accounting and professional experience in banking and financial operations, she cultivated discipline, stewardship, precision, and an appreciation for the systems that allow businesses to scale with excellence. Through technology and cloud engineering, she developed a passion for innovation, process design, automation, and scalable systems. Together, these experiences became the foundation upon which Pearls360 was built.

Today, she combines strategic thinking with creative vision to design experiences that are both emotionally compelling and operationally exceptional. Her passion for emerging technologies and visualization has positioned Pearls360 at the forefront of modern event design, incorporating architectural layouts, AI-assisted renderings, guest journey mapping, and immersive experience planning to help clients experience their events long before they come to life.

To Nifé, events are not simply productions. They are living stories. Stories told through movement. Through atmosphere. Through design. Through hospitality. Through memory. As Founder and Chief Experience Architect, her mission is simple: To transform imagination into grandeur while creating experiences that move people, strengthen brands, celebrate purpose, and leave a legacy long after the lights go down.

Because extraordinary events are never really about tables, flowers, or timelines. They are about people. And how those people feel long after the lights go down.

That is the heart of Pearls360. That is where imagination meets grandeur.

The Pearls360 Philosophy

We do not simply plan events. We architect them.

One intentional composition at a time.

Every experience begins with a private conversation and ends with a moment designed to live far beyond the occasion itself. From the cadence of a processional to the weight of a linen, every detail is intentional, every choice considered, and every element brought together with precision.

We believe extraordinary events are not simply seen. They are felt. They tell stories. They honor people, culture, purpose, and legacy. And long after the room is empty, the experience should remain.

We do not create for the moment alone. We create for the memory, the meaning, and the legacy it leaves behind.

We accept a limited number of engagements each season, allowing us to give every experience the depth, intention, and excellence it deserves.
