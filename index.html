The Pearls360 Philosophy

We do not simply plan events. We architect them.

One intentional composition at a time.

Every experience begins with a private conversation and ends with a moment designed to live far beyond the occasion itself. From the cadence of a processional to the weight of a linen, every detail is intentional, every choice considered, and every element brought together with precision.

Meet Nifé Kara

Founder & Chief Experience Architect

Nifé Kara is the Founder and Chief Experience Architect of Pearls360 Design & Planning, where strategy, hospitality, technology, and creative vision come together to architect extraordinary experiences.

With a professional background spanning accounting, banking, hospitality, business operations, and technology, Nifé brings a unique perspective to the world of events. Her foundation in accounting and experience in banking strengthened her understanding of financial management, business, and strategic decision making. Her time in the hotel industry shaped her appreciation for hospitality, service, and the guest experience. Later, her experience as a Cloud Engineer expanded her perspective on technology, systems, innovation, and architecture, teaching her how individual components can be thoughtfully built to work together as one.

Today, those experiences influence the way Nifé leads Pearls360.

For Nifé, an extraordinary event is about far more than creating a beautiful space. Every element, from the budget and planning to logistics, design, florals, production, technology, atmosphere, and the guest journey, should work together with intention. She considers not only how an event will look, but how it will function, how it will feel, and ultimately, how it will be remembered.

Her background in technology also contributes to the forward thinking approach she brings to Pearls360. By embracing emerging technology and AI assisted visualization, Nifé uses innovation as part of the creative process to explore possibilities, refine concepts, and help clients see elements of their experience before they are brought to life.

It is the combination of business, hospitality, technology, strategy, and creativity that has shaped Nifé’s approach as an Experience Architect. She brings structure to imagination, connecting every detail with intention to create experiences that feel cohesive, elevated, personal, and unforgettable.
